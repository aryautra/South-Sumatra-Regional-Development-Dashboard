Poverty, jobs, human development, inequality and output across the province and its 17 regencies and cities. Source: BPS Provinsi Sumatera Selatan statistical tables.

Green marks the better value (lower is better for poverty, unemployment and Gini; higher is better for HDI, GRDP and growth).

    Data and methods

    • Sources: BPS Provinsi Sumatera Selatan tables: poverty rate and number of poor people, open unemployment rate (TPT, August), HDI (IPM), Gini ratio, GRDP at current prices, and growth of GRDP per capita at constant 2010 prices.
    • Province GRDP is computed as the sum of the 17 regencies and cities. The growth series is per-capita growth and exists at regency/city level only.
    • Not included: inflation (the BPS table was monthly and too sparse), and GRDP by industry. Number of poor people starts in 2017.
    • Check before relying on them: a few extreme values kept as published (for example Empat Lawang 2020, OKI 2022 growth) and the sharp 2025 drop in Gini in many areas.
    • Limits: annual snapshots, no causal claims, and BPS may revise past figures.
